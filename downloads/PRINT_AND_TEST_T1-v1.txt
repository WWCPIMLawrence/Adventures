GATE TEST KIT T1-v1 (captured strips). Keeper-approved test only. Hard stop: Sun 22 Nov.
Push-and-turn gate with strips that can NOT fall out of the hub (each strip has a hook that slides under a ledge).

PRINT (one job, one bed if it fits):
  1. cover_T1-v1.stl           centre cover (gate version, smooth rim)   same filament as your earlier covers
  2. seat_T1-v1.stl            test seat (hub with the strip slots)      same filament as your earlier seats
  3. strips_T1-v1_soft07.stl   six PETG strips, THIN (0.7 mm)            PETG. This is the soft strip you used before.
  4. keeper_ring_T1-v1.stl     small loose ring, stands in for the inner ring   any filament, about 3 mm tall
  OPTIONAL: strips_T1-v1_firm08.stl  (0.8 mm, firmer). Print only if the soft ones feel too weak.
REUSE (do not reprint): the PETG retaining ring ring_O2-v1 (same part).
Write T1-v1 with a marker on the underside of the cover, seat and keeper ring. Put EVERY older centre part in the OLD box.

BUILD
  a. Press the PETG ring into the cover from underneath (as before).
  b. Strips: hold a strip with the ARCH UP and the two small feet DOWN. Slide it in from the OUTSIDE edge of the seat's
     centre ring, straight toward the middle, into one of the six slots, until it stops. Do all six. (Each has two hooks
     that go under a ledge. They only go in sideways.)
  c. Lay the keeper ring around the outside of the centre ring so it closes the open ends of the slots.
  d. Fit the knob with its M3 x 10 screw (zero fragments first).
  e. Drop the cover in, turn it clockwise about 28 degrees until it stops. It should sit slightly proud (about 1 mm).

TEST (hands only; tick each, write what happened on any fail)
  F. Strips stay in: remove the cover 10 times, turn the seat upside down and shake gently.   [ ] strips never moved or fell out
  A. ZERO fragments: unscrew the knob completely and screw it back, 10 times.   [ ] cover never turned  [ ] never lifted  [ ] never came off
  B. FOUR fragments: repeat 10 times.                                           [ ] cover never turned  [ ] never lifted  [ ] never came off
  P. Pressing on the knob while unscrewing it, 5 times, with zero fragments and then four.   [ ] cover never opened
  N. Normal use: pick it up, tilt and shake lightly, 5 minutes of handling.      [ ] cover stayed attached
  O. Deliberate opening: PRESS the cover down about 1 mm and turn anticlockwise 28 degrees, then lift. 10 times.   [ ] opened every time

STOP RULES: if ANY item fails, STOP. Do not print or change anything. Tell Cog the item letter, the cycle number, and what moved
(a short video helps). If a strip is stiff to slide in or will not go in, say so; do not file or force it.
If everything passes, tell Cog "T1-v1 passed".
