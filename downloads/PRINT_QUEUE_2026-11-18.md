## How the slots work
Each workday offers up to three printer slots. Each slot needs one swap by you (unload, wipe or check the bed, start the next job):
- **M (before work):** start about 6:30 AM (watch the first layers for 10 to 15 minutes while getting ready). It runs while you are away. **Only proven, single-material or proven two-colour jobs.**
- **E (evening):** start when you get home (about 6:15 PM). Attended for the first layers.
- **N (overnight):** start at bedtime, finished before the morning swap.
A short job simply finishes early and waits. **Nothing here needs a daytime visit.**

Times are estimates (slicer-generic / 4, calibrated from your two plates in 1:45). Colour-change time is included where marked. "Proven" means you have printed that part or geometry and it worked.

## Unattended-run checklist (M and N slots)
1. Check the AMS spools before you leave (the right colours, enough filament for the whole bed, no tangles).
2. Use the same profile as the proven prints; outer brim only on the plates (no brim inside holes).
3. Start the M job 15 minutes before leaving and watch the first layers. If anything looks off, cancel it and restart in the evening instead.
4. Keep M jobs under about 4 h where possible so a failure wastes little; none of the jobs above is longer than 2 h 45.
5. Leave the previous job's parts on the bed no longer than needed; after unloading, wipe the plate before the next job.
