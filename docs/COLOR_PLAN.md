# Final colour and hardware plan (owner-approved)

Rule: **brass = structure; black with ivory markings = turns; ivory with black digits = collect.**
Colours match the owner's actual spools (ERYONE brass-coloured PLA, matte black PLA, SUNLU Matte
Bone White PLA, dark PETG), not the concept image. The concept image is style reference only.

| Part | Body | Markings | How (0.20 mm layers) |
|---|---|---|---|

Cover colour was not specified: brass is assumed (structure). It only shows as the thin lines
between the fragments.

## Decisions on record

- **Index-cap arrow:** ivory on brass, approved. One cap is printed and inspected first. It must be
  clearly visible in normal indoor light. If the contrast is inadequate the owner sees the result
  before anything is changed to matte black.
- **Inner ring:** Option A. The ribs print in ivory with the symbols, as radial separators that
  echo the ivory numerals on the outer ring. Option B (a second filament change to keep the ribs
  black) is not used.
- No mechanical dimension, ring geometry, symbol, numeral or puzzle value changes.

## Screws

| Where | Screw | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Top, index cap (3) | Black pan-head Phillips self-tapping, about 3 × 16 | Keep the three uneven positions. No fourth screw, no Torx. A hex key does not open them |
| Back plate (4) | Black socket-head M3 × 8 | 2.5 mm hex key. Heads sit 0.2 mm below the surface |
| Centre knob (1) | M3 × 8 socket-head | Head is inside the knob bore; black preferred |

Twenty M3 × 8 socket-head screws are needed for four instruments (16 back plate + 4 knob).
