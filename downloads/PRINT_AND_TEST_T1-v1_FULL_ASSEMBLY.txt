T1-v1 FULL ASSEMBLY TEST (Keeper-authorised 20 Nov). One test unit only. NOT four-unit production approval.

PRINT (one part, alone on the bed):  deck_T1-v1.stl   same filament as your earlier decks.  Write T1-v1 on its underside.
Not needed: any PETG retaining ring (it only held the paper UV disc, which is dropped). Leave the ring out for this test.

USE FROM YOUR STOCK (mark which ones):  a base, the outer ring, the inner ring, the index cap and its 3 cap screws (M3 x 16),
the pawls (2 outer, 2 inner), the T1-v1 cover, the 6 soft 0.7 mm PETG strips (T1-v1), the fragments, the knob, and a NEW
M3 x 12 knob screw. (If a part is missing, say so; do not substitute silently. The back plate is optional for this test.)

BUILD ORDER MATTERS: the strips go in BEFORE the inner ring.
 1. Seat the deck on the base (as you did with your earlier decks).
 2. Slide the six strips in from the OUTSIDE edge of the hub, arch up and feet down, until each stops. Do not force any strip.
 3. Fit the inner ring over the hub (it closes the open ends of the strip slots), the outer ring, the pawls, then the index cap and its three screws.
 4. On the underside of the cover write SUN TO ARROW in UV marker, three lines, keeping about 5 mm clear of the centre (the screw tip hangs there).
 5. Fit the knob with the M3 x 12 screw. Drop the cover in and turn clockwise about 28 degrees until it stops.

TEST (hands only; write what happened for any fail)
 R. Rings: both rings turn with normal detents; no scraping or drag from the strips.             [ ] ok
 O. Press the cover down about 1 mm and turn anticlockwise 28 degrees, then lift. 10 times.       [ ] opened 10/10   [ ] re-seated 10/10
 U. Unintended release: with the cover locked, push down hard on the knob and unscrew it. 5 times. [ ] cover never opened
 A. ZERO fragments: unscrew the knob and screw it back, 10 times.   [ ] cover never turned  [ ] never lifted  [ ] never came off
 B. FOUR fragments: repeat 10 times.                                  [ ] cover never turned  [ ] never lifted  [ ] never came off
 K. Knob screw M3 x 12: how many turns to lift the knob far enough to place a fragment?  ____ turns
    Does the screw stay in its nut at that lift?  [ ] yes  [ ] no       Can all four fragments go in without taking the knob fully off?  [ ] yes  [ ] no
 F. Fragments insert and remove without catching.                                                  [ ] ok
 L. Underside: with the cover off and turned over, does the screw tip touch anything? Is the writing clear of the screw?   [ ] ok
 W. UV writing: still readable under UV after all the cycles above; invisible in normal light.     [ ] ok
 N. Normal handling: pick up, tilt and shake lightly for a few minutes; the cover stays on.        [ ] ok
 S. After everything, the strips are still in place and not drifting toward the inner ring.        [ ] ok

STOP RULES: if ANY item fails, stop. Do not print or change anything. Tell Cog the item letter, cycle number and what moved (a short video helps).
If everything passes, tell Cog "T1-v1 full passed".
