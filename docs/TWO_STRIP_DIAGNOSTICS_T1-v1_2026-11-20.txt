STRIP POSITIONS FOR THIS TEST (write down if you use anything else)
  Look from above with the double notch at 12 o'clock. Fit TWO 0.7 mm PETG strips at about 5 o'clock (148.6 deg clockwise from 12)
  and about 9 o'clock (271.2 deg). Leave the other four pockets empty (including the 1 o'clock pocket nearest the fixed arrow).
  (These are 122.6 deg apart, the widest pair of the earlier three.) Diagram: images_strip_positions_T1-v1.png

STEP 0, BEFORE THE RING GOES ON  (answers Keeper item 2)
  a. Push each strip fully inward toward the centre until it stops. Its outer end should be flush with the hub's outer edge or just inside it.
     Run a fingernail round the hub: no strip end should catch or stand proud.   [ ] strip 5 o'clock flush   [ ] strip 9 o'clock flush
  b. Optional: make a fine pencil mark on the hub top beside each strip's outer end, so you can see later if a strip has moved.
  Then fit the inner ring, pawls, outer ring, index cap and its three screws.

TESTS (hands only; tick, and write what you felt)
  1. RING ROTATION, cover REMOVED vs cover LOCKED (Keeper item 1). Turn the inner ring and the outer ring through several positions each time.
        cover removed:  inner ring spin  [ ] free  [ ] a little stiff  [ ] stiff    clicks  [ ] clear  [ ] faint  [ ] almost none
        cover locked:   inner ring spin  [ ] free  [ ] a little stiff  [ ] stiff    clicks  [ ] clear  [ ] faint  [ ] almost none
        outer ring (for comparison):  spin [ ] free [ ] stiff    clicks [ ] clear [ ] faint
        Is the 30-degree detent clear at every one of the 12 positions?   [ ] yes  [ ] no (which ones)
  2. WOBBLE (item 3). With the cover locked:
        normal handling (pick up, tilt, carry, turn the rings):  wobble or rattle?   [ ] none  [ ] slight  [ ] noticeable
        deliberately tapping the cover's edge at 4 points:       wobble?             [ ] none  [ ] slight  [ ] noticeable
  3. COVER LOCK AND RELEASE (item 4). Press down about 1 mm and turn anticlockwise 28 deg, lift, refit and re-lock. 10 times.
        [ ] opened 10/10     [ ] re-locked 10/10     [ ] any unintended release (when? ______ )
        Knob: unscrew and refit with zero fragments, 10 times  [ ] cover never turned/lifted/came off
              same with four fragments, 10 times               [ ] cover never turned/lifted/came off
              press hard on the knob while unscrewing, 5 times [ ] cover never opened
  4. RING SIDEWAYS PLAY (item 5). Cover removed. Gently push the inner ring sideways toward and away from a pawl, then the outer ring the same way.
        inner ring: play about ____ mm   [ ] similar to outer ring   [ ] more than outer ring   [ ] less than outer ring
  5. AFTER ALL THE ABOVE: strips seated? (item 2, item 5)  Remove the index cap and inner ring and look.
        [ ] both strips still flush with the hub edge   [ ] one has moved outward (which?) ______
        Any scuff, shiny rub marks or burrs on the strip ends, the hub's outer face, or the inside of the inner ring?   [ ] none  [ ] yes (where) ______
        Photograph: the hub with the two strips, and the inside of the inner ring.

DECISION RULES (Keeper's priorities)
  Reliable cover retention, clear 30-degree ring detents, smooth practical ring rotation, safe fragment operation, repeatable cover removal and refit.
  If all pass with two strips: tell Cog "two-strip passed"; Cog recommends a final physical acceptance test to Keeper.
  If wobble or interference is significant: STOP, tell Cog exactly what failed and what you saw. Do not change anything.
