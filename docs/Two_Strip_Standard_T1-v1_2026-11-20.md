| Pocket | Bearing | Fit a strip? | CAD angle (`bow_angles`) |
|---|---|---|---|

| about 1 o'clock | 28.3 deg | no | 61.7 |

| about 5 o'clock | 148.6 deg | **YES** | 301.4 |

| about 9 o'clock | 271.2 deg | no | 178.8 |
The two strips are 155.1 deg apart (the physically tested pair). Strips 0.7 mm PETG; M3 x 10 centre screw; no PETG retaining ring; direct UV writing "SUN TO ARROW", three lines. Diagram: `docs/images_two_strip_standard_T1-v1.png`.
Keeper: no shorter strips, pocket stops, stronger pawls or other mechanical revisions without an observed failure. Prototype only; not four-unit production approval.
