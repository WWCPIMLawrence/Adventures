# Cog to Keeper: T1-v1 full-assembly interim result (20 Nov 2026)
Owner-reported (PHYS), informal; not the counted acceptance data. CAD statements are predictions. No CAD or production geometry changed.

## Results so far
- **Stand-in ring removed:** the real inner ring now goes on the new deck (the earlier "ring too small" was the loose keeper ring still sitting on the hub).
- **Six strips:** the inner ring was very stiff on the deck; **three strips were damaged** (one broke while being installed, one was ruined pressing the ring on, a third broke on another attempt). Strips "want to pop out", especially at the bottom; once in, the inner ring holds them in.
- **Fewer strips, tested step by step:** 4 strips: ring spins better; 3: much better; 2: ring spins freely, cover locks, press-and-turn opens it, cover not falling out; **1 strip: not enough force to hold the cover.**
- **Three strips (one per pair): "work perfectly and balance it out really well"** (owner). The owner recommends three.
- The pawls, index cap and counted tests (O, U, A, B, K, F, L, W, N, S) are **not yet reported.**

## Cog interpretation (reasoning, not measured)
- The strips can drift outward in their open channel; the inner ring clears a seated strip by about 0.46 mm in CAD, so any strip sitting out drags on the ring. Six strips mean six places to drag. This is the risk flagged in the T1-v1 report ("strip migration / drag against the real inner ring"), now seen physically.
- Three strips, one in each gap between the cover's lug tracks (about 120 degrees apart), give a stable three-point lift. Two can let the cover tilt.
- Installation is fragile: the strips are thin soft PETG (0.7 mm) with small hooks.

## Proposed configuration for your approval (no geometry change)
**Three strips per instrument, one per pair, same side of each pair, in the existing six-pocket deck.** Unused pockets stay empty. This lowers strip handling and parts (12 plus spares instead of 24 plus spares for four instruments) and reduces ring drag. **Not yet proven:** the counted test sheet, with the pawls and index cap fitted (they add detent resistance), and long-term drift/rubbing against the ring. If drift is confirmed, a small end stop in the channel would be a design change; I will not start it without your approval.
